# NewMap Humanoid Visuals

Player and NPC visuals are lightweight runtime primitives:

- Cylinder body.
- Sphere head.
- Two cylinder legs.
- Player material: blue.
- NPC material: orange.

No heavy character assets are used. Materials are created at runtime with URP Lit fallback to Standard to avoid missing magenta materials.

Final status: `completed_with_documented_runtime_proxy`.
