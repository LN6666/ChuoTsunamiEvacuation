# P10-B Light Curtain Visual Smoke

P10-B does not reimplement the P8 risk front or light curtain.

Manual playtest should verify:

- light curtain appears after hazard start when enabled
- sweep direction and coverage are plausible against the P8 handoff model
- `visualHeightMeters` remains cinematic-only and is not described as physical tsunami height
- green ground frames remain visible with the light curtain enabled
- ResultPanel and controls are not obstructed
- performance impact is recorded with light curtain enabled and disabled

Final high-detail visual/performance evidence remains a P10-C/P10-D release-candidate item.
