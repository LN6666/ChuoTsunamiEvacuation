# P8 Final Known Limitations

Date: 2026-05-24.

- `Assets/Scenes/P7HighDetail/P7_HighDetail_Chuo.unity` is the practical high-detail baseline, but it still has known LOD/category limitations.
- P8 official hazard layer v1 uses extracted Tokyo/Chuo tsunami samples. Derived boundaries are not full official inundation contours unless separate official contour evidence is proven.
- P2-P6 adaptation is smoke/proxy level, not final release gameplay.
- Humanitarian high-rise candidates are non-official and require public access, management agreement, entrance, safe-floor, and seismic/manual review before future gameplay use.
- Damage and collapse are proxy visual/status outputs, not engineering predictions.
- There is no real indoor scene gameplay because no LOD4/BIM indoor scene exists.
- P9 owns final real gameplay landing.
- P10 owns release packaging, archive, and final handoff packaging.
