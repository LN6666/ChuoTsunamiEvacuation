# P9-B Known Limitations

- Runtime marker placement uses proxy Unity coordinates or simple WGS84 proxy projection. It is not proven one-to-one PLATEAU scene-object binding.
- Humanitarian high-rise candidates remain non-official and require warnings. P9-B does not make them final selectable evacuation targets.
- P5 route/candidate geometry remains estimated prototype guidance. P9-B does not claim official evacuation routes or road-geometry validation.
- Crowd behavior is lightweight and capped. It is not a full crowd simulation.
- Collapse/debris zones are warning/status markers only. They do not kill the player and do not mutate outcomes.
- Entrance blocked, congestion, safe-floor, evacuation-complete, and failure outcomes remain P9-C scope.
- Dynamic risk front and light-curtain coordination hooks are data/runtime-ready only. Final visual QA remains P10.
- Windows EXE profiling and release packaging remain P10 scope.
