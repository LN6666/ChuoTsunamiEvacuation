# P9-D Route And Geometry Limitations

P9-D improves route/candidate usability through coordinate-based proxy anchoring.

Remaining route limitations:

- routes are estimated prototype guidance
- routes are not official evacuation routes
- routes are not fully road-geometry validated
- WGS84 / Unity / PLATEAU transform limitations remain
- route passability under hazard or blockage is not officially validated
- nearest-road proxy matching is gameplay guidance, not official route evidence

Remaining geometry limitations:

- coordinate anchors do not prove exact Unity scene-object identity
- coordinate anchors do not prove exact PLATEAU Unity object identity
- nearest-building anchors do not prove official PLATEAU object identity
- safe-floor proxy anchors do not prove real floor access
- entrance proxy anchors do not prove real entrance geometry

These limitations are P10/future QA items, not P9-D blockers.
