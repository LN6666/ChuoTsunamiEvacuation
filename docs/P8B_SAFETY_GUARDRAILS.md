# P8-B Safety Guardrails

P8-B remains a bounded dynamic risk-front visualization stage. It must preserve the existing serious-game prototype behavior while making the tsunami risk-front visual clearer.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- The visual curtain is cinematic only.
- No full fluid simulation is introduced.
- No gameplay success/failure changes are introduced.
- No road/building/bridge/underground interactions are introduced here.
- No collapse proxy gameplay is introduced before P8-D.
- No P9 systems are introduced.
- No P10 systems are introduced.
- `P7_HighDetail_Chuo` and `Chuo_BaseMap` are not modified.
- `ProjectSettings` and `Packages` are not modified.

## Science Versus Visual Safety

`visualHeightMeters` exists for readable visual scale. It is not an official tsunami height, water level, inundation depth, or hazard evidence value.

`sourceMode` remains limited to controlled test, manual sample, evidence-planned, or reviewed official metropolitan tsunami evidence states. Even when Tokyo Metropolitan Government evidence is identified, P8-B must not claim a complete official Chuo spatial inundation surface until extraction is reviewed.

## P8 Stage Boundary

P8 has exactly five stages:

- P8-A
- P8-B
- P8-C
- P8-D
- P8-E

Do not create P8-0, P8-F, or P8-G.
